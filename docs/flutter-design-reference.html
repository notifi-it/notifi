The identity in one line

A terminal that happens to be an app

The whole product is one red bell and a monospace typeface on white. No cards, no gradients, no chrome it doesn't need. The credentials you paste into curl are the hero — shown big, in orange, tap to copy. It reads like a dev tool because it is one.

Why this doc exists. The SwiftUI rebuild currently uses stock ContentUnavailableView — a generic grey bell and "No notifications yet." That throws away the entire personality below. This is the look to bring back: Inconsolata, the sad bell, credentials in orange.

What made it feel like notifi

Monospace or nothing

Inconsolata for every label, title, and number. It's the single biggest signal that this is a tool for people who live in a terminal.

The bell is the brand

One red bell (#bc2122), centered in the bar, with the unread count tucked at its shoulder. No wordmark needed.

Credentials are the hero

Shown big, orange, tap-to-copy — on the empty state and in settings. The thing you actually came for is never more than one tap away.

The sad bell has feelings

An empty inbox draws a frowning bell, not a shrug. Personality where iOS ships a blank. This is the detail the stock rebuild dropped.

Stark white, hairline greys

Pure white ground, #eae8e8 hairlines, no shadows or cards. Structure comes from rules and weight, not elevation.

Two-button bottom bar

Mark-all-read and delete-all, split by a single red hairline. The two things you do to a list of alerts, and nothing else.
