Six native-iOS directions for the feed

Same skeleton in every one — three pages on a bottom tab bar (Feed · Keys · Settings), and the feed you already have. What changes is how a feed row looks and, above all, how a long message hides and reveals. Every phone below is live: tap the long "Deploy failed" message to see that option's reveal.

If you want a steer

Option 2 (Banner) is the most on-brand: the feed row is the notification, and expanding in place mirrors the Lock Screen — a notifications app that feels like notifications. Option 1 (Mail) is the safest and most familiar. Option 6 (Peek) gives the richest actions for the least chrome.

Whichever you pick, the long-message rule is the same choice underneath: expand in place (2, 3, 5) keeps you in the feed; go somewhere (1 push, 4 sheet, 6 menu) gives the full message its own space. Tell me which one and I'll build it into the real SwiftUI app.
