notifi sends push notifications to your iPhone or Mac from anything that can make an HTTP request — a shell script, a cron job, a CI pipeline. Notification content is encrypted with your public key, so neither we nor Apple can read your notifications, and each one is deleted from the server once delivered. No account, no SDK: the app makes you a send key on first launch. Free and open source, on the App Store for iOS 17+ and macOS 14+.
