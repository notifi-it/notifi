No account, no sign-in. The app mints a send key on first launch, so a reviewer
can test it without credentials from us.

To see a notification arrive:

1. Open the app. The empty inbox shows two numbered steps. Step 1 is "Allow
   notifications" — tap "Enable notifications" and accept the system prompt.
2. Step 2, "Send one", shows a curl command with this device's own key already
   in it. Tap "Send a test" to send that exact request from the app itself; the
   push arrives within a few seconds and appears in the list.
3. "Copy" puts the same command on the clipboard, so it can also be run from any
   terminal to send from outside the app.

Message content is encrypted with your public key. We cannot read it, and
neither can Apple.
