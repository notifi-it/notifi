> The terms for using the notifi apps and service. Using either means you accept them.

_Last updated 25 September 2026_

## The service

notifi turns an HTTP request to `notifi.it/send`, authenticated by a send key, into a push notification on the device that created the key. The service is free and has no accounts: your device’s keys are your identity, as the [privacy policy](https://notifi.it/privacy) describes.

Maximilian Mitchell, based in the United Kingdom, runs the service. Contact: [hello@notifi.it](mailto:hello@notifi.it).

## Your keys and your device

- A send key delivers only to the device that created it. You are responsible for where you put your keys and for what the scripts holding them send.
- If a key leaks, revoke it in the app. Sends made with it before revocation still count as yours.
- Deleting the app deletes your identity, and you cannot recover it.
